The steering wheel

The steering wheel has worn to a shine under your hands. No stretch of road in Chongqing is straight, including the one that goes down the mountain.

You lived on floor 1, under the bridge. Traffic passed overhead — every so often the floor trembled, and after a while you could tell a light truck from a loaded one. When you could not sleep, you counted.
