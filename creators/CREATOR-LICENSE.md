## The one-line pitch

> A Date with Chongqing — a browser life simulator where you pick the floor you live on, and the floor decides your life. One life. No re-rolls.

## Why the floor thing is the whole game

If your video doesn't mention it, most viewers will assume it's a generic stat-heavy life sim. The floor is the hook: you choose a vertical layer (L0 underground to L7 tower) and it rewrites your commute, your health, your rent, your relationships, and the ending you get. If you pick floor 24 you will climb stairs for the rest of your life. If you pick L0 you live half-underground. The game does the arithmetic — you don't have to explain it.
